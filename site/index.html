Most tools make slides. Quorum makes a room take part.

A poll, a quiz, an estimate or a question is a slide in the talk, not another tool to switch to. People join with their phone's camera and the answers appear on the screen as they arrive.

01 How it works

Agent, Studio, present

You describe the talk, you adjust what you like, and you give it. Quorum takes care of the rest.

01

Ask your agent for the talk

Open your coding agent in the talk folder and say what you want, the way you would ask a colleague. It writes the slides, checks that they look right and fixes what it finds.

your agent, in the talk folder

Make me a 15-minute talk on why bread rises. Start with a poll, add a quiz on yeast and end with questions.

Wrote 16 slides, 3 where the room answers

Ready. Open it with npm run studio

02

Tweak it in Studio

Studio shows the real slides. Click to change a word, drag in a poll or a chart, or try a slide in another layout. For bigger changes, press Ask agent and your agent takes it from there.

  • Every edit is written to talk.md
  • Checks catch what will not fit before the day
  • Rehearse with a simulated audience

03

Present it

Press Present. The slides go on the big screen, and your laptop keeps the presenter view: your notes, what comes next, how many people are connected and the questions waiting for approval.

No audience? Export the same talk as a PowerPoint file or a PDF instead.

05 Why Quorum

Not another slide editor

Quorum has no AI and no servers of its own. It works with the agent you already use, on your own computer.

Your agent builds the talk

Start from a description, not a blank slide. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any coding agent writes the talk, checks every slide and changes it when you ask. Colleagues can work on it too. How

The room becomes part of it

Polls, quizzes, estimates and questions are slides in the talk, not a separate tool to switch to. People join with their phone's camera.

It's yours

Open source under the MIT licence. The talk is a Markdown file on your computer, the answers stay there, and you can add your own components. The audience never needs an account.

06 Get started

Your first talk

You need Node.js 18 or newer and a coding agent. It takes a couple of minutes.

  1. 1

    Make a talk folder

    $ npx github:JBoixCampos/quorum new my-talk
$ cd my-talk
$ npm install
  2. 2

    Ask your agent for the talk

    Open your agent in the my-talk folder and describe the talk: the topic, how long, and where the room should answer.

  3. 3

    Open Studio

    $ npm run studio

    Tweak the slides, then press Present.
